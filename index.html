Privacy Policy for GrooveCircle

Effective Date: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the GrooveCircle mobile application ("the App") handles user information.

1. No Personal Data Collection

GrooveCircle is designed as an offline rhythm and metronome utility. The App does not require user registration or login, and we do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, location, contacts, or device identifiers).

2. Local Storage on Your Device

All custom rhythm presets, tempo settings, beat accents, and slot configurations created within GrooveCircle are stored strictly locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer. Uninstalling the App or clearing its app data removes all locally saved settings.

3. Third-Party Services & Analytics

GrooveCircle does not integrate third-party advertising networks or behavioral tracking SDKs.

4. Device Permissions

The App only uses standard audio playback capabilities required to generate metronome clicks and rhythm pulses. It does not record audio via your microphone or access your camera, files, or contacts.

5. Children's Privacy

Because GrooveCircle does not collect any personal information from any users, it complies with applicable children's privacy regulations.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if new features are added to the App. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the App, please contact us at: groovecirclesupport@gmail.com.
